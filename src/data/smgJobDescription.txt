Job Description
Your Mission 💡

As a Domain Architect for the Ricardo Platform, part of the General Marketplace Business Unit, you will own and evolve the architectural vision of Switzerland’s leading online auction marketplace. This is a high-impact role where you will shape the future of a platform handling close to CHF 1 billion in transactions annually.

You’ll translate business strategy into scalable technical design, help teams implement architectural best practices, and ensure cohesion across contexts like listing, selling, buying, and payments.

We’re on a journey to modernise a beloved platform — and we need your expertise to guide it.
 

Specifically, you will:

Design and evolve the Ricardo domain architecture using Domain-Driven Design (DDD)
Define subdomain structures and ensure architectural coherence across teams
Translate business goals into a long-term technical roadmap
Collaborate with Team Architects, Engineering Managers, and Product Leads to align strategy and delivery
Lead domain-wide modernisation initiatives such as authentication revamps or event-driven architecture
Evaluate new technologies, run PoCs, and introduce innovations that improve developer experience and system scalability
Promote a culture of learning by mentoring engineers and facilitating architectural workshops
Your responsibilities will evolve as we grow.

 

Qualifications
What will you bring to the ride? 🚀

Significant experience in software architecture roles, ideally within large-scale platforms
Strong foundation in Domain-Driven Design and building decoupled systems
Hands-on knowledge of cloud-native tech (preferably GCP, Google Cloud Platform), microservices, and distributed systems
Strategic mindset and ability to align technical direction with business goals
Excellent communicator who can drive consensus across teams and stakeholders
Passion for mentoring and raising architectural maturity across the organisation
We welcome all career paths — if you’re passionate about building great systems, we want to hear from you!
